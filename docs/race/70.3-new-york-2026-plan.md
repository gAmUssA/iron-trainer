## 1. The course is not what it advertises

Marketing calls it "lightning-fast, flat, fully closed." Two independent sources say
otherwise, and the difference changes how you ride it.

**Swim — 1.2 mi, Zach's Bay, counter-clockwise.** Protected bay, salt water (buoyant).
The shelter is real, *usually*. In 2023 conditions were bad enough that organisers
**shortened the swim to 750 yd**. Plan for a normal swim; do not be thrown if it is cut.

**Bike — 56 mi, Wantagh Parkway, fully closed, out-and-back ×2.**
- The race guide claims **750 ft** of gain. A finisher's Garmin recorded **2,000 ft**.
  Barometric drift on a flat coastal course probably explains most of that gap — but
  two separate reports describe a **"near constant steady incline" from Jones Beach out
  to the Jericho Turnpike turnaround**. Treat it as gently but persistently uphill
  outbound, downhill back. Twice.
- **Wind is the dominant variable.** 2023 saw 25 mph sustained with 30+ mph gusts. An
  exposed barrier-island parkway in late September sits in the tail of hurricane season.

**Run — 13.1 mi, Jones Beach Boardwalk, two loops.** Flat, oceanfront, and **no shade
anywhere**. Two loops means you pass the finish before you are done — a known morale
hazard.

## 2. Bike power — ride softer than the app tells you

**Iron Trainer projects your bike at IF 0.78 (≈197 W). Do not ride that.**

That constant is tuned for a well-trained 70.3 athlete. At **CTL 59.6** you are not
carrying the durability to hold 0.78 for 3:45 and still run. The projection is a
*fitness estimate*, not a prescription — this is the one place the app's number and
your race plan should disagree, and it is why this document exists.

| | IF | NP | use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Target** | **0.72** | **181 W** | ride here |
| Ceiling | 0.75 | 189 W | brief, into wind/incline only |
| Never | 0.78+ | 197 W+ | the app's projection — not your plan |

- **Variability Index under 1.05.** A pancake-flat closed course gives you no terrain
  forcing recovery, so surges are pure unrecovered cost.
- **First 20 min: cap at 170 W.** You will feel fresh and the road is flat. Everyone
  goes out too hard here.

### The wind rule — the highest-leverage sentence here

**Into the wind, hold power and let the speed go. With the wind, do not chase.**

The blow-up on this course is watching your speed collapse on the outbound leg and
adding 25 W to "fix" it. You cannot fix wind with watts; you can only spend your run.
Outbound is likely both uphill *and* into the wind, and **you do it twice**.

If anything: +5 W outbound, −5 W homebound. Not ±25.

## 3. Run — the first 3 km decide the race

Projected **2:04:56 (5:55/km)**. That is 110% of your 5:23 threshold pace off a 3:45
bike — achievable *only* if the bike was ridden at 0.72.

| segment | pace | note |
|---|---|---|
| km 1–3 | **6:15–6:20** | deliberately, uncomfortably slow |
| km 4–16 | 5:55–6:05 | settle here |
| km 17–21 | whatever is left | earned, not planned |

Your legs will feel good for the first kilometre off the bike. That feeling is a lie
told by fresh blood flow and it lasts about 8 minutes. Running 5:30s early costs
minutes later — every time.

Two loops, no shade: take fluid at **every** aid station even when you do not want it.

## 4. Fuelling — math shown, estimates flagged

**Carbohydrate**

| leg | duration | rate | total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bike | ~3:45 | 70 g/h | 263 g |
| Run | ~2:05 | 50 g/h | 104 g |
| | | | **≈ 367 g** |

70 g/h is a deliberately moderate start because **`gi_tolerance` is unset** — nobody
has tested what your gut takes at race intensity. Trained guts handle 90–120 g/h;
untested ones do not, and GI distress is the most common 70.3 race-ruiner.

**Fluid — ESTIMATE, and the weakest number in this document**

`sweat_rate_l_h` is **null**. Placeholder: **0.8 L/h** for a 100 kg athlete at ~20 °C.

- Bike ≈ 3.0 L · Run ≈ 1.7 L

For a 100 kg athlete the true figure could plausibly be 0.6 or 1.4 L/h. **At 1.4 you
would finish the bike 2 L down and the run would not happen.** §5 replaces this with a
measurement.

**Sodium — ESTIMATE** ~600 mg per litre → **≈ 2.8 g** total. Raise if it is warm or you
finish rides visibly salt-crusted.

## 5. Do this in the next 10 days: sweat-rate test

The one input you cannot derive from Strava, and it must happen before taper.

1. Weigh nude, empty bladder.
2. Ride 60 min at ~180 W, ideally in similar warmth to race morning.
3. Log every mL drunk.
4. Towel off, weigh nude again.

`sweat rate (L/h) = (kg lost) + (L drunk)`

Then set `sweat_rate_l_h`, `gel_carb_g` and `gi_tolerance` in Settings and this plan's
§4 stops being an estimate. Do the same session with your **actual race nutrition** —
test the gut and the fluid in one go.

## 6. Runway — 32 days

CTL 59.6 with TSB −12.3 and ACWR 0.64. You are fresh and slightly detrained, not
fatigued. **Hold CTL; do not chase it.** Fitness bought in the last month arrives as
fatigue, not form.

| week | focus | volume |
|---|---|---|
| **Aug 25–31** | Build. Race-sim brick: 2:30 bike @ 181 W + 20 min off at 6:00/km. | ~11 h |
| **Sep 1–7** | Peak. Longest ride, 3:00–3:30 @ race power. **Sweat-rate test here at the latest.** | ~11–12 h |
| **Sep 8–14** | Last real week. Full race-nutrition rehearsal on the long ride. | ~10 h |
| **Sep 15–21** | Taper. Volume −30%, keep intensity — short, sharp, frequent. | ~7 h |
| **Sep 22–26** | Race week. Volume −50%. Openers Thu/Fri: 20 min easy + 3×1 min at race power. | ~3 h |

Target race-day **TSB +5 to +15**. Negative on race morning means the taper did not
work; above +25 usually means it went too far and you feel flat.

## 7. Race morning

- **Weather check the night before.** If wind is forecast >20 mph, drop the bike target
  to **0.70 (176 W)** and add 3–4 min to the bike expectation. It is a wind tax, not a
  failure.
- Swim may be shortened. Do not let it disrupt the rest.
- Projected total **6:38:19** against an 8:30 cutoff — **+1:51 of margin**. You are not
  racing the clock. That margin is permission to ride 0.72 rather than 0.78.
