Added — Your plan is just there when you open the app

• No more tapping "Load my plan". The Today view loads itself at launch, and again every time you bring the app back to the foreground, so it's never showing yesterday's session after the phone has sat overnight.
• It also works with no signal. The last plan is stored on the device, so Today is on screen instantly at launch — before the network answers, and even in airplane mode.
• "Load my plan" is still there for the genuine first-run case and as a manual retry.

Test on device
1. Cold start: force-quit the app, reopen it. Today should appear immediately — no "No workout yet", no tap, no visible spinner if you already had a plan.
2. AIRPLANE MODE (the important one): turn on airplane mode, force-quit, reopen. Today should still show your plan. You should NOT get an error screen — if the app can't reach the server and has nothing stored, you get the normal empty screen with a short reason on it, never a dead end.
3. Overnight: leave the app backgrounded overnight, then reopen tomorrow. Today should have rolled over to the new day's session on its own.
4. Opening a .itw file (Files / Mail / AirDrop) while the app launches should keep you on that workout — the automatic load must not yank you into the plan mid-preview.
5. Sign out (Settings → Sign out). Your plan should disappear from the app AND from the Home/Lock Screen widgets, and morning briefs should stop. Re-pair and it should come back.
6. Re-pair to the same server without signing out first (scan a fresh QR) — you should end up on your own current plan, never a stale one.

Notes
• Applying a fitness test on the web now re-targets your upcoming workouts too, so a new FTP should show up in the bike power targets on this build's plan — previously it only changed your thresholds.
• The app makes one extra plan fetch each time it comes to the foreground. If you notice any battery or data oddity, say so.
• Still testable from previous builds: native Apple Health sync (Settings → Apple Health) and the Readiness widget.
